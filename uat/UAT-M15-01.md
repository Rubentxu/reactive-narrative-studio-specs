# UAT-M15-01 — Digital publishing: EPUB reflowable + fixed-layout + validation adapters

## Given
The same accepted chapter (M12 manuscript + its SequentialArtIR projection) and the two M15 EPUB 3 export targets, each validated by the deterministic validation adapter (reader-entry contract) before download.

## When
The publishing proof view (`/publish`) builds both EPUBs and the tester downloads them.

## Then
Both artifacts satisfy the reader-entry contract that reference readers check on open: `mimetype` as first stored entry with exact content `application/epub+zip`; `META-INF/container.xml` resolving to `OEBPS/content.opf`; an EPUB 3 package with unique identifier, dc metadata and dcterms:modified; a nav TOC; manifest hrefs and spine idrefs resolving inside the archive. The reflowable EPUB carries the chapter prose and dialogue as real text; the fixed-layout EPUB declares `rendition:layout=pre-paginated` with `svg` page properties, viewport metas, and its embedded pages keep the layered comic SVG (editable lettering, no raster).

## Pass
- The validation panel on `/publish` shows `ok` for both variants before download; download buttons are disabled while validation fails.
- The downloaded reflowable EPUB: mimetype first+exact; container→OPF; nav links to the scene chapters; chapter XHTML contains beat prose and verbatim dialogue (searchable in the stored bytes).
- The downloaded fixed-layout EPUB: mimetype first+exact; container→OPF; pre-paginated rendition meta; one page document per IR page with embedded `comic-lettering-layer` SVG text and no raster image tags.
- Both downloads are standard store-mode zips verifiable by bytes.

## Does not prove
- Rendering quality inside specific third-party reader applications (structural reader-entry contract only — no epubcheck/JVM conformance claim).
- Print preflight rules (M9 target), Kindle packages or webtoon export (later targets per doc 14).
- Reflow typography quality (the proof proves structure and content fidelity, not visual typesetting).
