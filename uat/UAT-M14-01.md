# UAT-M14-01 — Dual-render proof: comic vs manga presentation profiles

## Given
The SequentialArtIR of an accepted chapter (M12 adapter output — the SAME source document) and two declared presentation profiles: `comic` (LTR, flat fills) and `manga` (RTL, monochrome, screentone).

## When
The studio proof view (`/comic`) renders the chapter through each profile and exports a CBZ proof per profile.

## Then
The same sequential source renders through distinct presentation profiles: manga reads right-to-left (panel 0 mirrored to the rightmost column of its row), uses a monochrome (grayscale-only) palette with a dot-grid screentone pattern in the art slots, and preserves the identical editable lettering of the comic render.

## Pass
- The manga render declares `data-direction="rtl"` / `data-profile="manga"`; the comic render declares `data-direction="ltr"` / `data-profile="comic"`.
- Manga panel placement mirrors the comic grid: the first panel's x is greater than the second panel's x (2-column row reads right→left); the comic profile keeps panel 0 leftmost.
- Every `fill`/`stroke` color in the manga page SVG is grayscale; the comic page keeps its M13 palette.
- Manga art slots are filled through the screentone `<pattern>`; the comic page contains no pattern.
- Narrative lettering text is identical across both renders (same dialogue, editable `<text>`, never baked).
- Both CBZ proofs are standard store-mode zips carrying profile-marked pages plus `ComicInfo.xml`.

## Does not prove
- Real image generation (art slots remain placeholders).
- Typographic quality of manga lettering or authentic tone work (deterministic pattern, doc 11 defers optimization).
- Third-party CBZ viewer compatibility.
