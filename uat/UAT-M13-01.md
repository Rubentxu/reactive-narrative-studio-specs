# UAT-M13-01 — Comic proof with layered composition

## Given
The SequentialArtIR of an accepted chapter (M12 adapter output) and the comic presentation profile.

## When
The comic renderer produces a comic proof (CBZ) from the layered page model.

## Then
The proof is layered: an art layer (placeholder slots tagged with shotRefs — image generation comes later) and a separate lettering layer (balloons/captions as editable `<text>`); the CBZ contains one vector page per IR page plus `ComicInfo.xml`.

## Pass
- Dialogue exists as editable text nodes in the lettering layer (searchable in the stored CBZ bytes) and is never baked into raster/generated images (the proof contains no `<image>` and no rasterized text).
- Toggling the art layer off does not remove the lettering (separation by construction).
- The CBZ opens as a standard store-mode zip with the declared entries.
